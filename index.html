Setup
Connect your Google Sheet
Step 1 — Paste your Google Sheet CSV URL The site reads data from a published Google Sheet. Paste the CSV export URL below and click Save.
 
How to get your Sheet URL 1. Open your Google Sheet
2. File → Share → Publish to web
3. Choose the Results tab, select Comma-separated values (.csv)
4. Click Publish and copy the URL
5. Paste it above. The site will refresh data every time you reload.
Expected Sheet column format The site expects two sheets published as CSV:

Sheet 1 — Results (gid=0)
match_id | home_team | away_team | group | date | result
result values: home / draw / away / blank

Sheet 2 — Picks (gid=1 — auto-filled by Google Form)
timestamp | player_name | match_1 | match_2 | ... | tournament_winner | golden_boot

See the README for the exact Google Form setup and column mapping.
